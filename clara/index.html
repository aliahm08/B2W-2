Clara / Concept phase

Complete tasks on job sites.

Clara is a customized, private workspace concept where project teams could capture information, develop documents, complete tasks, and review work directly from the job site.

Explore capabilities ↗See how it works ↗

What Clara could do

Give document work a dedicated home.

Bring field notes and company standards into a focused workspace for scopes, estimates, and proposals. Every output stays editable and subject to review.

Capture

Describe the job in plain language or start with notes from the site.

Apply standards

Use approved pricing, preferred vendors, formats, and review rules to structure the work.

Review

Check scope, costs, assumptions, and revisions before a document is ready to use.

How it works
