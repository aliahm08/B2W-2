FAQs

Questions we expect you to ask.

How is JasonAI different from ChatGPT?
It can respond anywhere you already send messages (WhatsApp, iMessage…) and it can connect to sources like Salesforce and Procore. Plus, we don’t use your data for public purposes.
Where does JasonAI get answers from?
That is a core part of the product design. Where relevant, JasonAI should point back to the software, file, message, or project record behind the answer so the context can be checked.
Does JasonAI take actions without me?
The level of automation depends on the workflow. For higher-impact actions, the safer product direction is to keep the action visible and reviewable rather than hide it behind the system.
Why pay for JasonAI instead of using a free tool?
Our model is based on delivering enough operational value that the product is worth paying for. That keeps the focus on product quality, usefulness, and the customer relationship.
